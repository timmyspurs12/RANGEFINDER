Recommendation: A — The Instrument

Why it wins: it is the only direction whose central metaphor is the product’s own name — Rangefinder is literally an instrument for bracketing a distance. Its estimation screen gives continuous, perceptible feedback (rolling counters over a ticked drum), which is what “actively making a prediction” feels like; its reveal is mechanical storytelling (truth needle → gap bracket → sweeping gauge → counter) rather than a big isolated number; and its ivory/brass/black palette is unmistakable in Reddit’s feed without leaning on empty dark backgrounds. B donates warmth (its tape/pin reveal ideas can soften A’s drum) and C donates rigor (its “C = ?” dimensioned-artwork idea could replace A’s free tape end if you want the artwork to be even more literal). Any blend is one CSS/SVG pass — none of this needs new dependencies or WebGL.
