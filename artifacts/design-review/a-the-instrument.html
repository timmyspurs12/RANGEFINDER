IDENTITY — “THE INSTRUMENT”

  • The app literally becomes its name: a precision surveying instrument. Ivory-enamel housing, machined brass controls, black measuring drum, porcelain readouts, one indicator red. Dark surfaces are dense and functional (a drum), never an empty void.
  • Material depth comes from machined layering — bezels, knurled grips, rivets, inset windows, hard drop shadows that read as physical weight. No gradients used decoratively; shading only models material.
  • Typography: engraved serif caps for the brand and actions, tabular numerals for every reading. Numerals are always inside an instrument surface (porcelain counter, drum chip), never floating labels.

INTERACTION APPROACH

  • The slider is a measuring drum: 54×164 px brass thumbs with knurled grips, amber luminous window between them, fine ticks that make every 1-px drag perceptible.
  • Live values roll in porcelain odometer counters joined by brass hinge rivets — reading the instrument, not filling a form. Lock is a full-width brass lever with real travel (6 px press).

MOTION

  • Drag: thumb depresses 3 px, amber window follows instantly, counters flip values.
  • Reveal: truth bezel drops onto the drum and settles with a slight overshoot; on a miss a red hatched bracket extends from your edge to the truth with the ×factor; gauge needle sweeps ~1.5 s with ease-out while the counter counts to the exact server score (respecting prefers-reduced-motion in production).

EMBEDDED FIT (719×545)

  • Splash budget: nameplate 46 + question 118 + instrument 240 + CTA block 132 = fits with air; nothing clipped, one CTA, no labels overlapping the artwork, no internal scroll.
  • Game budget: question row 84 + counters 84 + drum assembly 175 + lock 94 + how-to 30. Controls occupy ~65% of the frame.
Why this is the recommendation: the instrument metaphor is Rangefinder’s own name, the drum gives continuous tactile feedback no quiz template can copy, and the reveal is mechanical storytelling (needle → truth → counter) instead of a big number with small labels.
