IDENTITY — “THE DRAFTING ROOM”

  • Every daily challenge is an engineering drawing: cyanotype ground with a real blueprint grid, white construction line-work, sheet border with crop marks, and a title block that carries genuine information (sheet number, scale) — not a badge.
  • The splash artwork is a technical drawing of the question: a wireframe sphere whose equator is dimensioned “C = ?” with proper extension lines and arrows. The illustration IS the question’s notation.
  • Typography: condensed sans (drawing lettering) for headlines, stenciled monospace for every numeric, safety-orange reserved for the dimension you control.

INTERACTION APPROACH

  • The slider is a drafted dimension line: orange crosshair handles drop extension lines up to stenciled value chips; the selected band is hatched like a section cut. Dragging rotates the crosshairs into an “X” — the drafting feel of taking a measurement.
  • Lock = STAMP DIMENSION — flat, heavy, square-cornered, with real press travel.

MOTION

  • Drag: crosshair rotates 45°, chips and extension lines track the handle, hatch band follows.
  • Reveal: a white dimension arrow drops onto the sheet with the true value boxed; on a miss a red hatched delta zone fills between your edge and the truth with “Δ ×1.60”; an inspection stamp slams in rotated; score counts to the exact server value against a 0–100 rule.

EMBEDDED FIT (719×545)

  • Splash: title block 40 + problem head 130 + figure 232 + CTA row 100 — fits; the only text on the artwork is the drawing’s own notation.
  • Game: header 80 + dimension area 186 + lock 86 + how-to 26; 44 px handles, chips pinned above handles, nothing overlaps.
